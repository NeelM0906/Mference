# Bounded Flux

*An algorithmic philosophy for the Mference mark*

## The movement

Bounded Flux is the aesthetic of infinity passing through a finite window. Its
subject is not the shape of a thing but the discipline of its flow: a vast
population of possibilities streaming toward a small, fixed aperture, where
only a chosen few are admitted, and where what is admitted — briefly, brightly
— does all the work. The composition is never drawn; it condenses. Hundreds of
dim currents cross the frame, and from their disciplined selection a single
luminous form precipitates, the way a fixed working set precipitates meaning
out of a model too large to hold.

## Computational expression

The philosophy manifests as a field-and-particle system with three strata. At
the outer boundary lives the reservoir: many streams — an order of magnitude
more than will ever glow — born on a rim at seeded angles, each a candidate
that may never be called. Between rim and center lies the passage: a vector
field blending curl-like Perlin turbulence with an inward attraction whose
strength grows as radius shrinks, so chaos at the edge resolves into intent at
the core. At the center stands the attractor: a sparse skeleton of segments
that the admitted streams trace and re-trace until their accumulated light
becomes a letterform. Nothing ever strokes the glyph directly. It exists only
as a bias in the field, made visible by ten thousand small arrivals.

## Selection and decay

Two mechanisms give the system its soul. First, selection: from the full
population, a seeded handful of streams are elevated — brighter deposit,
longer life, greater conviction — while the rest remain near-subliminal
texture. The image's hierarchy is therefore not composed but *routed*. Second,
decay: every trace fades by a constant exponential factor per step, so the
picture is a perpetual negotiation between arrival and forgetting. The bright
form persists only because it is continually re-earned; the moment flow stops,
the mark begins its slow return to ink. Equilibrium, not completion, is the
finished state.

## The ring

Where passage meets center, a segmented annulus records history. Each segment
counts the crossings it has witnessed, and its luminance follows that count —
a frequency memory rendered as architecture. The ring is the only geometric
element permitted to be drawn explicitly, because it is the only element of
the system that *is* explicit: a fixed number of slots, never growing, lit
unevenly by whatever the flow actually favored. Runs with different seeds
light different segments; the structure is constant, the memory never is.

## Craftsmanship

Bounded Flux tolerates no accidental beauty. The turbulence octaves, the
radius at which chaos yields to attraction, the decay constant, the deposit
alpha of the elect versus the mass — every parameter must read as though it
survived hundreds of discarded tunings by a master of the form, because a
system this simple exposes every lazy choice. The mark must condense within
seconds yet keep breathing forever; the faint population must be felt rather
than seen; the glyph must be unmistakable at thumbnail size and inexhaustible
at full scale. That balance — legibility from chaos, meticulously earned — is
the movement's signature, and anything less reads as noise.

## Seeded variation

Every seed is a different admission history: different streams elected,
different ring segments warmed, different turbulence weather over the same
bounded window. The identity survives all of them. That invariance under
variation is the point — the window is fixed; the flux is infinite; the mark
is what the discipline of the boundary makes of it.
