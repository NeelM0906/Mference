Mference menu bar

A MenuBarExtra scene inside the existing Mference app, beside its main window. It shows what the engine is doing, switches and unloads models, follows downloads and updates, and opens the window. Every row reads a field the app already has; rows marked NEW need one more. Figures are examples; sizes are the app's own.

Main menu

One app, two doors. The window stays the main surface. The menu bar is for a glance and a quick action, and it keeps working when the window is closed.
Engine first. The top row is EngineServiceStatus: phase, resident or target model, a generation running and requests waiting.
Fit before load. Each model shows its MemoryFit verdict and estimated memory, as Settings › Models does.
Native glass. macOS 26 draws the menu material, highlight and dark mode. Reduce Transparency gives a solid menu.
