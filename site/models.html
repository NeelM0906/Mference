Getting a model

Pick it, download it, switch any time.

  1. 1

    Pick one on first run

    The app helps you choose a model when you first open it, and recommends Quick for every Mac.

  2. 2

    Download in the app

    Quick, Deep and Bonsai download from inside the app, which shows their size and the memory they need before you load them. The other five install from the terminal, and the app finds them on its own.

  3. 3

    Switch any time

    Choose the model for each chat from the composer.
