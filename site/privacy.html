Privacy

What stays on your Mac? All of it.

There is no Mference account and no Mference server. The model, your index and your memory are files on your disk. Here is every way the app touches the network, and how to turn each one off.

Where your data goes

Offline mode

On this Mac, always

  • The model and every answer it writes
  • Chats, memory and Skills
  • The Find index and its search model
  • Notes, recordings and transcripts
  • Scanned receipts and documents

Leaves only when you ask

  • Web searchThrough the search engine bundled in the appWhen asked
  • Mail and calendar syncYour own providers, through Apple Mail and CalendarAs today
  • Model downloadsPinned files from Hugging Face, checked by SHA-256When asked
  • Speech modelsDownloaded when you set Notes upOnce

In practice

Concrete, not promised.

Nothing sent without you

Drafted replies and proposed events wait for you. A reply is not sent until you press Send; an event is not added until you add it.

Trackers removed

Mail is read as text with tracking pixels removed, and the message tells you how many it found.

Memory you can read

Everything the assistant remembers is listed in one place. Review it, and remove what you don't want kept.

Private chats

Mark a conversation private and it is never used for memory.

Asked at first use

Calendar, Reminders, Mail and Full Disk Access are each requested when you first open the feature that needs them, never at install.

Open to inspection

MIT licensed. Every line that reads your data is on GitHub, and the bundled search engine ships with its source.

Privacy questions

Does Mference send my chats anywhere?

No. The model runs on your Mac and there is no Mference server to send them to. With web search on, the search query goes out through the bundled search engine; the conversation itself does not.

Can I use it with no network at all?

Yes. Turn on Offline mode and the app stops all network use. Chat, Find, Notes and Scan keep working with the models you already have.

Where are my files indexed?

In a database on your Mac, built by a small search model that also runs on your Mac. Photos, video and audio are indexed only if you turn them on.

How do I report a privacy problem?

Follow the security policy to report it privately.

Found something that leaves when it shouldn't?

We'd rather hear it from you than read it in the code later.
